Two developments stand out from 2021.

First, a group of senior OpenAI staff, led by siblings Dario and Daniela Amodei, left to start
their own company, Anthropic, set up that year with a focus on AI safety <Ref n={9} />. Their
worry was that AI was moving faster than anyone's ability to make it safe and predictable, so they
wanted a lab where safety research and frontier development sat side by side. Anthropic would go
on to build the Claude family of AI assistants and a training method it called
"constitutional AI" <Ref n={9} />. The split said something about the field too: arguments over
how fast to move, and how much caution to apply, would keep reshaping who worked where.

Second, OpenAI showed transformers weren't only for words. Its DALL-E system, announced in January
2021, could take a written description, say "an armchair shaped like an avocado", and produce an
original image to match <Ref n={10} />. The machines were starting to create, not just read and
write.
